Corvallis, Ore. and Knoxville, TN – NuScale Power Corporation (NYSE: SMR), the industry leading provider of proprietary and innovative advanced small modular reactor (SMR) technology, and MillenniTEK, an advanced nuclear materials manufacturer, today announced that they have successfully fabricated first-of-a-kind boron-oxide pellets for NuScale’s passive emergency core cooling system (ECCS).
