CORVALLIS, Ore. – Framatome and NuScale Power Corporation (NYSE: SMR) have expanded their longstanding partnership to include Framatome’s European facilities for fuel fabrication in Europe, while also notifying the Richland facility to fabricate fuel for NuScale Power’s U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC)-approved SMR fuel technology in the next five years.
